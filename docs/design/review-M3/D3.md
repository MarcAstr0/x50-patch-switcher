# D3 design review, M3 (browse, settings) at 1x, 1.25x, 2x

Compared `browse`, `browse-search`, `settings`, `settings-errors` at `@1`, `@1.25`, `@2`
against DESIGN.md and the two mockups. Measured with Pillow on the PNGs (px are logical
unless marked device). Accepted deviations from the brief are not listed.

Verified correct (no action): pixel-font LCD strip is exactly `480k x 74k` device px at all
three scales (bezel 16..495 x 16..89 at 1x; 20..499 x 20..93 at 1.25x, k = 1; 32..991 at 2x,
k = 2); at 1.25x the only non-two-level pixels in the strip are the four bezel corners, so
the glyphs are crisp (DESIGN §2.2 k = 1 holds). Token colours sampled and exact: selected row
`#1E4A5C`, accent `#5DB0D0`, panel `#1B2024`, field `#101417`, separator `#2A3238`, star
`#F0B65A`, splitter 6 wide. Toolbar y 102..141 (40), strip 74, gap 12, status line centred in
its 24. Focus ring on search, selected row and current-slot triangle present. No text is
elided or clipped anywhere in the six browse and six settings captures except the
`Programs` toggle crowding (item 2).

## Deviations, most visible first

### Medium

1. **Set editor slot grid has the wrong geometry** (browse, all scales). Measured at 1x:
   cells are 58 high (y 888..945 and 954..1011), 12 below the header row (header ends 876)
   and 16 above the panel bottom (1028); DESIGN §7.9 and the mockup give 62 high, 8 gap, 12
   bottom. Horizontally the cells start at x 238 and end at 1888 while the header row spans
   234..1892: a 4 px inset each side, so the first column is 407 wide against 408.5.
   Fix (DESIGN §7.9, now written): grid flush with the header row edges, top = header
   bottom + 8, last row bottom = panel bottom - 12, row height 62, gap 8, no extra inset;
   focus rings overflow into the panel padding. Builder: remove the 4 px layout margin
   (it was probably for the ring) and the extra 4 above and bottom.

2. **`Programs` toggle is cramped** (browse, all scales). Toggle 1350..1449 (100 wide):
   check mark 6 px from the outer edge, text ends 5.5 px from the other edge, touching
   the 2 px border visually; `Combis` and `GM` have air. Fix: toggles 112 wide (fixed),
   constant left padding 20 (12 check + 8 gap), text centred in the rest; gives 10 px and
   11 px at `Programs`. DESIGN §7.3, §7.5 and `app.qss` updated; Theme `kindToggleWidth` =
   112, `kMarkReserve` = 20, check left = text left - 20.

3. **Toolbar widths** (browse, all scales). `Read names` measures 126 (DESIGN said 120, text
   needs 94 + 32), set editor buttons measure `Rename` 96 and `Duplicate` 106 (DESIGN said
   84 each; `Duplicate` does not fit in 84), `Next` 90 against `Prev` 88. Fix: DESIGN now
   states the rule `max(listed, advance + 32)` and the values `Read names` 126, `Rename` 96,
   `Duplicate` 106, `New` 84, `Delete` 84, `Prev` and `Next` 90. Search field at the
   minimum window is 262 (min 260), checked.

### Low

4. **QSS borders are soft at 1.25x** (browse, settings, 1.25x). Search border column at
   x = 600: rows `#366274` (blend), `#5DB0D0`, `#5DB0D0`; settings button: `#2B343A`
   (blend) then `#46525A`; slot cell `#2C343B` after `#46525A`. Edges fall on 127.5 and
   similar half device px. 1x and 2x are exact (1 and 2 device px, no blend). DESIGN §3.5
   promised snapping everywhere. Decision written into §3.5: strict for QPainter items,
   accepted for QSS controls (a one-row blend of a 1 px border, text unaffected). No code
   change. Owner can veto, which would mean custom-painting every control.

5. **Prev / Next chevron spacing** (browse, all scales): gap chevron to text is about 5 px
   on `Prev` and 3.5 px on `Next` (mockup 10). Fix (§7.8): chevron 7 x 12, 2 px stroke,
   gap 8 on both, group centred.

6. **Manual channel and Bank Map state** (settings vs settings-errors): disabled with a
   passing check, enabled otherwise. DESIGN was silent. Now written (§8): disabled only
   while the last check found the X50. Owner to confirm.

7. **Close button placement** (settings, all scales): the build puts it below the card
   (x 384..504, y 619..659, 16 below the card), the mockup inside the card. §8 said "bottom
   right". Now written as below the card, right edge on the card edge (matches the build).

8. **QSS content-box sizes** (code, not visible): `app.qss` wrote 40 and 88 as content
   sizes, which Qt grows by the border. Now 38 (1 px border) and 110 for the toggle, header
   section 27, rule in §10. Header comment tokens fixed (`lcd-bg`, `lcd-text` removed).

## Observations that are not deviations

- Body text in the offscreen captures has coloured subpixel fringes (for example
  `#A6632A` / `#4C83B8` at the edge of settings labels). DESIGN requires grayscale only for
  the pixel font, so this is platform default and is left alone. If the owner dislikes
  ClearType on stage, set `QFont::NoSubpixelAntialias` on body fonts: not required.
- Output combo is narrower than Input (the Test button takes the space); matches the mockup.
- `settings` at 1x shows a plain `1` in a disabled spin; as item 6.

## Second look

Re-measured `browse@1`, `@1.25`, `@2` with Pillow (settings captures are byte-identical to
the first pass, so items 6 and 7 stand as the build; item 4 unchanged by decision).

1. **Slot grid: resolved.** Cell rows y 884..945 and 954..1015 (62 high each, 8 gap),
   header bottom 875 so 8 below it, panel bottom 1027 so 12 below the last row. Cells span
   x 234..1891, flush with the header row; columns 408 + 8 gap. Same at 2x.
2. **`Programs` toggle: resolved.** Toggles 1210..1321, 1326..1437, 1442..1553: 112 wide,
   gap 4, y 102..141. At `Programs` the check ink starts 12 px from the outer edge, text ink
   ends 10 px before the far edge (spec 10 / 11, within the glyph side bearings); `Combis`
   and `GM` have more air. Same at 1.25x and 2x.
3. **Toolbar widths: resolved.** `Read names` 126 (1562..1687), `Settings` 100 (1696..1795),
   `Live` 100; `Prev` 222..311 and `Next` 320..409 both 90; `Star` 1816..1903 = 88;
   `New` 84, `Rename` 96, `Duplicate` 106, `Delete` 84, `6` and `8` 36 each, gap 8.
4. **Soft QSS borders at 1.25x: accepted by decision, no change** (still one blend row on
   1 px borders, e.g. cell edge x 1067.2).
5. **Prev / Next chevrons: resolved.** Chevron ink 7 wide; empty gap to the text 9 px on
   both (chevron ink 243..249, text 259; text ends 374, chevron 384..389), symmetric, within
   a pixel of the spec 8 (text side bearing).
6. **Manual channel / Bank Map state: resolved** (written in §8; settings captures unchanged,
   consistent with it). Owner confirmation still open.
7. **Close placement: resolved** (matches §8; capture unchanged).
8. **QSS content-box sizes: resolved in the file, now corrected further.** The first fix was
   wrong for the toggle: Qt adds `padding-left` as well. `app.qss` now has
   `min-width: 90px` (112 - 20 - 2 x 1px) and `:checked { min-width: 88px; min-height: 36px }`
   (112 - 20 - 2 x 2px; 40 - 2 x 2). DESIGN §10 reworded. The widget-level override in
   `KindToggle.cpp` (and the `6 | 8` toggles in `SetEditor.cpp`, content 34 / 32 with
   `padding-left: 0`) can be removed once the builder confirms the toggle still measures 112.

Leftovers settled:

- **Slot-cell focus ring inside the cell: accepted.** DESIGN §7.9 now says 2 px ring, outer
  edge 2 px inside the cell edge, within the 8 px padding. Not visible in the twelve
  captures (no slot cell is focused), so unverified by pixel: capture one with a focused
  cell next round.
- **Star icon 16 px with Qt's gap: accepted.** Measured ink 12.5 wide, about 5 px to the
  text (Prev/Next are 9), group 19.5 px from the left edge and 21.5 from the right. Slightly
  tighter than the chevrons, not visible at arm's length; §7.8 amended.

New deviations: none.
