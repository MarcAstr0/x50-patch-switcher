# D4 design review, M4 live view at 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2x

Compared `live-8`, `live-6`, `live-disconnected` at `@1`, `@1.25`, `@1.5`, `@2` (all
1920x1080 device px, so `u` = 1.5, 1.2, 1.0, 0.75 and `U` = 1.5 throughout) against DESIGN
§2.2, §3.5, §5.2 to §5.7, §6 and the two live mockups. Measured with Pillow on the PNGs.
All px below are device px. 1366x768 was not captured, so the table in §5.3 for that
screen is unchecked here.

## Verdict on the ampersand

Judged on the shipped screenshots: yes, the old Bold `&` read as `8` with a notch at
`k` = 6 ("Piano &Strings", all four scales, identical pixels), and at `k` = 3 in the top
bar readout. Cause: closed lower loop, 6 px wide, 1 px counters, so the silhouette matched
an `8`. Redrawn (item 1). New `&` is 7 px wide with the right side open (arm at the upper
right of the lower half, separate tail bottom right). Checked beside `8`, `6`, `S`, `B`
at `k` = 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2 in `docs/design/mockup/glyph-test-amp.png`: distinct at every
size, and at `k` = 6 `&Strings` reads as an ampersand.

## Deviations, most visible first

Counts: 0 High, 1 Medium (fixed here), 1 Minor (builder), 1 DESIGN correction (done).

### Medium

1. **Bold `&` reads as `8`.** State: live-8 slot 3, `Piano &Strings`; also any name or
   readout containing `&`. Scale: all (the name pixels are identical at all four). Measured:
   glyph 6 px wide cols 1 to 6, counters 1 px, closed lower loop; side by side with `8`
   at `k` = 6 only the right-hand notch differs. DESIGN §2.2. Fix, done: `patch_font.py`
   Bold `ampersand` replaced (7 px wide, cols 1 to 7, advance still 8 px, open right side),
   `assets/fonts/PixelOperatorX50-Bold.ttf` regenerated (verified with fontTools: `ampersand`
   is the only glyph that differs from the previous file; advance and unitsPerEm unchanged,
   so §5.3 fits and widths are unchanged), §2.2 text updated. Builder: nothing to change in
   C++, rebuild so the new font is embedded; re-shoot `live-8` to confirm. Regular `&` is
   unchanged (used only for `--` and browse; reads as an ampersand at 32 and 48 px in
   `glyph-test.png`).

### Minor

2. **Strokes at 2x are 1 px thicker than the spec.** State: all live states. Scale: 2x only
   (`u` = 0.75). Measured: lit border 10 px (spec 6u*dpr = 9), normal border 4 px (spec 3),
   top bar rule 4 px (spec 3); at 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x they are 9, 3, 3 exactly. Same family:
   address line 230 px wide at 2x against 225. DESIGN §3.5 and §5.4. Cause: the width is
   rounded to logical px first (4.5 becomes 5, 1.5 becomes 2) and then doubled. Fix
   (builder): compute stroke device width as `max(1, round(w_design * u * dpr))`, i.e.
   `round(6 * U)` and `round(2 * U)`, never round to logical px. The address font may stay
   as is (fractional logical size rounded, accepted by §5.1). Visible effect is small
   (a heavier border), so no re-shoot is needed beyond the 2x lit capture.

## DESIGN correction

3. **§5.4 note said nothing else is bright.** The LCD readout field `#BFC3BA` has relative
   luminance 0.54, above the lit cyan fill `#5DB0D0` at 0.38. The lit border `#F2F5F7`
   (0.91) still makes the lit button the brightest object, and the field is only 685 x
   54 px in the bar, so no value changes; the note is corrected and logged in §12
   ("Change round D4").

## Verified correct (no action)

- Grid at all scales: top bar 84 px high (83.75 at 1.25x), gap 30, rows 429 px (428 to 430
  by rounding), row 1 starts y 114, last row ends y 1001 to 1002, margin 36, gap 30. 8
  slots: columns 440, 440, 440, 438 (spec 440, 440, 440, 438; 1.5x 440, 439, 440, 439). 6
  slots: 596, 596, 596 (+-1). Name box 392 x 219 (8) and 548 x 219 (6).
- Name sizes `k` measured as the gcd of ink run lengths: 8 slots Intro pad 7, Verse EP 7,
  Piano &Strings 6, StereophonicKeys 3, Waiting4Sunrise 3; 6 slots all six names 7. All
  match `fit_table.py` and the §5.3 table, identical at every scale (ink pixel counts equal).
- Crispness: every name box holds exactly two colours (`#1A1F24` and `#F2F5F7`, or `#5DB0D0`
  and `#04141B` when lit) at all four scales. Lines are centred within +-1 px of the box,
  baselines align across a row (names without descenders sit up to 10 px high by the
  12-row rule, as written).
- Lit signals: 9 px `#F2F5F7` border (spec 9), fill `#5DB0D0`, dark badge 66 x 66 (spec
  66), `NOW` tag with a solid triangle about 20 to 21 x 22 to 24 (spec 21 x 24) and cap
  height 23 px, right edge 2 px inside the inner box. Three non-colour signals present.
- Empty slot: no fill, dashed `#2A3238` border (dash 12, gap 9 = 8u, 6u), dashed badge,
  `EMPTY` centred in the whole button, `#7E8A94` text.
- Readout: bezel 691 x 60 (40u), field inset 3, ink segments 48 high (32u), ink inset 6

  26 px gap (16u = 24), dotted zero visible in `PROG 0:Play` and `D104`.
- Connection: LED 24 px, text starts 19 px after it (12u = 18), right margin 38 (24u = 36);
  disconnected shows a red ring with bar and `NO X50` in `red`, lit button
  kept, nothing else differs from live-8 (bar diff only).
- Esc hint: centred at x 959 to 960, key cap 36 px high (24u), vertically centred in the
  strip. Text width varies 661 (1x, 1.5x), 684 (1.25x), 686 (2x) because the 18u font size
  rounds to a whole logical px; inside §5.1 rounding, no action.

## Legibility at arm's length, plainly

Nothing else should change. The lit button (cyan fill, 9 px white border, inverted text,
`NOW`) is unmistakable; the names at `k` = 6 and 7 (54 and 63 px caps) are large and
crisp; the 16-character names at `k` = 3 (27 px caps) are the weakest names but are
legible zoomed (`StereophonicKeys`, `Waiting4Sunrise` checked glyph by glyph, `K`, `W`,
`4` distinct). Two smaller points for the owner, not deviations:

- The mode segment `PROG 0:Play` is the smallest dot-matrix text (`km` = 2, caps 18 px,
  about 3 mm). The program or combination kind is also on each button's address line, so
  it is a confirmation, not the primary reading. Left as specified.
- 1366x768 was not captured: `k` = 2 there for 16-character names (owner choice already
  in §5.3). Worth a real-laptop check at stage distance.
